# How to Use the Specimen Index

A quick guide for students and teachers browsing the 3D biology models — no technical knowledge needed.

## Opening the site

Just visit the site link in any browser — phone, tablet, or laptop. Nothing to install.

## Browsing specimens

The home page is a list of available specimens (e.g. Animal Cell, Mitochondria). Tap or click any entry to open it in 3D.

## Looking at a model

Once a specimen is open:

- **Rotate** — drag your finger (or mouse) across the model to spin it around.
- **Zoom** — pinch in/out on a touchscreen, or scroll on a laptop, to get closer to a part.
- **It rotates on its own** when you're not touching it, so you can just watch if you'd rather not interact.

## Reading the labels

Small pins on the model mark different organelles.

1. Tap a pin.
2. A panel pops up naming that part and explaining what it does, based on your class 9–10 textbook.
3. Tap the ✕ to close the panel and keep exploring.

## Viewing it in AR (phones only)

Look for the AR icon near the model. Tap it, point your phone at a flat surface (a desk or floor works well), and the model will appear life-sized in front of you — you can walk around it like a real object. This works on most recent iPhones and Android phones; older devices may not support it.

## Where the models came from

Full credit to the original 3D artists is on the **Credits** page, linked at the bottom of the home page.

## Trouble viewing a model?

- Make sure you have a working internet connection — models load fresh each time.
- If a model looks stuck or blank, try refreshing the page.
- AR not showing up? It only works over `https://` links (not `file://`) and needs a fairly recent phone.
